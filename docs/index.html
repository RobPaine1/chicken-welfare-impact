Chicken welfare calculator

I ate oz of cooked chicken.

Step 1. How much of a chicken is that?

cooked chicken eaten oz = g
÷ cooking yield (meat loses about a quarter of its weight when cooked) ÷ 0.75 = g of raw meat
÷ raw meat from one chicken ÷ g = of a chicken ()

Where "raw meat from one chicken" comes from:

live weight of a U.S. meat chicken 61.1 billion lb ÷ 9.33 billion birds = 6.55 lb = g
× dressing (carcass as a share of live weight) × 0.75 = g carcass
× edible meat as a share of the carcass × 0.74 = g of raw meat

Step 2. How much did that chicken suffer?

The Welfare Footprint Project estimated how many hours an average conventional meat chicken spends in pain over its life (about 47 days), cause by cause. Your meal's share is that number multiplied by the fraction of a chicken from step 1.

cause of pain hours per chicken × your fraction = hours behind your meal

The same hours, split by how bad the pain is instead of what caused it:

intensity per chicken × your fraction = behind your meal

The two tables come from different releases of the same data and differ by about 4%. Treat the by-cause numbers as proportions rather than exact hours.

Sources

  1. Live weight: USDA NASS, Poultry Production and Value 2024 Summary (9.33 billion broilers, 61.1 billion lb).
  2. Dressing 0.75: University of Maine Extension, Understanding Poultry Yields.
  3. Edible share 0.74: University of Wisconsin Extension, Bird Breakdown.
  4. Cooking yield 0.75: USDA Table of Cooking Yields for Meat and Poultry.
  5. Life of 47 days: National Chicken Council, U.S. Broiler Performance.
  6. Hours of pain by intensity: Welfare Footprint Institute, Broilers, conventional scenario. Consistent with their finding that the Better Chicken Commitment prevents 33 h of Disabling (66%), 79 h of Hurtful (24%) and 25 s of Excruciating (78%) pain per bird.
  7. Hours of pain by cause: Welfare Footprint Institute, Pain-Track for broilers, "time in pain per harm" for the average bird.
  8. Pain intensity definitions: Welfare Footprint Institute.

Not included: pain during transport and slaughter, breeder birds, and diseases the project did not model. The numbers are conservative.
